# Voice Recording Guide (for SpaceXAI Application)

SpaceXAI's JD asks for **clear, natural vocal delivery** in Chinese and English. Record these samples on your phone or computer in a **quiet room**, then upload to this folder or Google Drive and link in your resume.

## Recording tips

- Distance: 15–20 cm from microphone
- Format: `.wav` or `.m4a`
- Avoid: fan noise, echo, background music
- Speak naturally—not too fast, not robotic
- Do 2 takes, keep the better one

## File naming

```
voice-samples/
├── xueyi_gao_chinese_reading.wav
├── xueyi_gao_english_reading.wav
└── xueyi_gao_chinese_news_tone.wav  (optional)
```

## Script 1: Chinese reading (~60–90 seconds)

朗读下面这段，语气自然、清晰，像新闻播报但不要太僵硬：

```
人工智能正在改变我们与计算机交互的方式。语音技术让用户可以通过说话来完成搜索、提问和控制设备。

然而，要让机器准确理解不同口音、语速和语言环境下的语音，仍然面临许多挑战。背景噪声、同音字和说话者的情绪变化，都会影响识别结果。

因此，高质量的语音数据标注至关重要。标注员需要仔细聆听每一段录音，准确记录说话内容，并在遇到模糊或歧义的情况时做出一致、可解释的判断。

我希望通过自己的工作，帮助多语言语音系统变得更加可靠，为更多用户带来自然流畅的语音交互体验。
```

## Script 2: English reading (~60 seconds)

Read clearly at a natural pace (IELTS 7.0 level is fine):

```
Artificial intelligence is transforming how people interact with technology. Voice interfaces allow users to search, ask questions, and control devices simply by speaking.

However, building reliable speech systems remains challenging. Accents, background noise, homophones, and variations in pacing can all affect recognition accuracy.

High-quality human annotation is essential. Annotators must listen carefully, transcribe exactly what is spoken, and make consistent judgments when audio is ambiguous.

I am interested in contributing to multilingual voice AI systems that work reliably for users around the world.
```

请仔细听这段录音。（停顿半秒）
在语音标注工作中，细节决定数据质量，而数据质量决定模型的表现。
